Where this plan is tight

  • 8 Aug — Rotterdam to Maria Laach is 2h45, then another 4h45 on to Munich. With the abbey stop that's a full 8-hour day, on day one. Hence the bed.
  • 10 Aug — the Vršič Pass is 80 km and takes 2 hours on fifty hairpins. Beautiful, slow, hard on EV range.
  • 17 Aug — Kotor to Žabljak, and whatever's actually reliable to charge on once there blocks the day 13 routing choice entirely.
  • 23 Aug — Salzburg (or wherever day 15 lands) to Rotterdam in one push, deliberately past the usual comfort ceiling — and with the buffer day traded for an extra Durmitor night, there's no recovery day after it either.
