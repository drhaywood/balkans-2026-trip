Vršič & the Soča

Over the pass early, down the far side to the river, home before the coals are ready. Around 4 hours driving, 3 hours walking, and one very cold swim.

    Izvir Soče — the source

    890 m · 45 minutes

    Where the whole river starts, straight out of the rock. Easy path to the viewpoint. The last stretch to the spring itself is cabled and genuinely exposed — no shame in stopping short. Very few parking spaces, so we may have to wait for one.

  13. 20 min

  14. 15:55

    Velika korita — the Great Soča Gorge

    450 m · 1 hour 30

    Luminous turquoise water in a limestone slot. Walk the length of it, scramble the slabs, swim if you dare — the river runs 9–11 °C all summer. The rock is polished and slick near the edges.

Two decisions on the day

Turnaround · 12:30

Not on the summit by 12:30? Turn back. The whole afternoon depends on being at the car by 14:00. The meadow is lovely but it isn't worth losing the river for.

Hard cutoff · 18:00

Leave Velika korita by 18:00 at the absolute latest. The plan has us going at 17:25, so the margin is thinner than it looks — this is the full version of the day, with nothing dropped.

Worth knowing

  • Storms build from about 2pm over these mountains in August. That's exactly why the walking happens in the morning — we'll be off the ridge before anything develops. If the forecast looks angry, we swap: river first, hike after.
  • The drive home is the leg that slips. Two hours assumes moving traffic. Late afternoon on Vršič means coaches, campervans and cyclists on fifty hairpins with nowhere to pass.
  • If we're running behind, the lever is the gorge — cutting it to an hour puts us home by 18:55. Don't cut the hike; we'd have driven all that way for it.
  • No signal in upper Trenta. Download the offline map for the region before we leave.
